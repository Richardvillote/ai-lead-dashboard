Introduction

What is the AI Lead Dashboard?

A full-stack web application that helps you capture, organize, track, and close business leads — all in one place.

🌐

Public Landing Page

A professional website your potential clients visit. They fill out a contact form, and their information is instantly saved to your dashboard.

📊

Private Dashboard

A password-protected admin panel where you manage every lead, track progress, schedule appointments, and log calls.

🤖

Smart Lead Scoring

Every lead gets an automatic score (0–100) based on their information and activity, so you always know which ones to follow up with first.

📧

Instant Email Alerts

The moment someone submits the form, you receive an email notification so you can respond fast — which dramatically increases conversion rates.

The Lead Journey

How a Lead Moves Through the System

From first contact to closed deal — here's the full path a lead takes.

🆕 New
Just submitted
📞 Contacted
Reached out
⭐ Qualified
Serious prospect
✅ Closed
Became a client
1

Visitor fills out the contact form

On your landing page (localhost:3000), a potential client enters their name, email, phone number, the service they're interested in, and a message.

2

Lead is saved to the database instantly

The form submission hits the API, which stores all their details in the database. Their status is automatically set to New.

3

You get an email notification

An email is sent to your inbox (configured in .env) with the lead's full details and a direct link to the dashboard. The faster you reply, the better your conversion rate.

4

You review the lead in the dashboard

Log in to /dashboard/leads, find the new lead, click on it to see their details, score, message, and history — all in one side panel.

5

You contact them and update the status

After calling or emailing the lead, change their status from New → Contacted. Log the call outcome directly in the dashboard with one click.

6

Schedule a follow-up appointment

Book a discovery call, meeting, or product demo directly from the lead's detail panel. It appears in /dashboard/appointments so you never miss it.

7

Qualify the lead and close the deal

Move the lead to Qualified when they show serious intent, then to Closed when they become a paying client. Your conversion rate updates automatically.

Feature Guide

Every Feature Explained

What each part of the dashboard does and why it matters for your leads.

📊

Dashboard Overview

Your command center. Shows 4 key stats (Total Leads, Conversion Rate, Qualified Leads, New This Month), a 14-day trend chart of incoming leads, a pipeline funnel showing where leads are stuck, and your 6 most recent leads.

✓ Spot trends before they become problems
👥

Leads Table (with Search & Filter)

A full list of every lead. Search by name or email. Filter by status (New, Contacted, Qualified, Closed). Change a lead's status directly from the dropdown in the table row — no need to open the lead first.

✓ Find any lead in under 3 seconds
🔥

Lead Scoring (Hot / Warm / Cold)

Each lead is automatically scored 0–100 and labelled Hot, Warm, or Cold based on: whether they left a phone number, the length and quality of their message, which service they're interested in, how far along the pipeline they are, whether they have appointments or calls logged, and how recently they submitted.

✓ Prioritize your time on the leads most likely to close
✏️

Edit Lead Details

Click the pencil icon on any lead to open the edit modal. Update their name, email, phone number, service, and add internal private notes that only you can see. Notes appear highlighted in the detail panel as a reminder.

✓ Keep your data clean and up to date
📞

Call Logging

Click "Log Call" on any lead and record the outcome: ANSWERED, VOICEMAIL, or NO_ANSWER. Every call is timestamped and stored. View the full call history in the lead's detail panel or in the dedicated Call Logs page.

✓ Never forget who you've called or what happened
📅

Appointments Scheduling

Schedule a Phone Call, In-Person Meeting, or Product Demo for any lead. Set the title, date/time, and duration. View all upcoming appointments on the Appointments page, and mark them Completed or Cancelled after they happen.

✓ Stay organized and never miss a meeting
📁

Export to CSV

Click "Export CSV" on the Leads page to download all your lead data as a spreadsheet. Includes name, email, phone, service, status, message, notes, appointment count, call count, and date. Ready for Excel, Google Sheets, or your CRM.

✓ Share reports or import data into other tools
🔐

Password-Protected Dashboard

The entire /dashboard section is protected by a proxy layer. Anyone who visits /dashboard without being logged in is automatically redirected to /login. Sessions last 7 days. Logout from the sidebar at any time.

✓ Your client data stays private and secure
📧

Email Notifications

Powered by Nodemailer. Every time a new lead submits the form, an email is sent to your inbox with their full details and a link to the dashboard. Configure your Gmail or any SMTP provider in the .env file.

✓ Respond within minutes — conversion rates drop fast after 5 mins

Understanding Lead Scores

The scoring system helps you instantly know which leads deserve your attention right now.

🔥
Hot Lead
Score 65–100 · Follow up immediately
🌡
Warm Lead
Score 35–64 · Follow up within 24h
❄️
Cold Lead
Score 0–34 · Nurture over time

How the score is calculated:

Signal Points Added Why it matters
Phone number provided +15 Shows willingness to be reached directly
Message length > 80 chars +20 Detailed messages show genuine interest
Message length 20–80 chars +12 Some effort to explain their need
Service interest selected +10 They know what they want
Status: Contacted +15 You've already made contact
Status: Qualified +28 Confirmed as a real prospect
Status: Closed +40 Already a paying client
Has scheduled appointments +8 Active engagement
Has logged calls +7 Ongoing communication
Submitted within last 3 days +10 Fresh leads are hottest
Submitted within last 7 days +5 Still relatively fresh

Best Practices

Tips to Close More Leads

Use the dashboard to its full potential with these proven habits.

Tip 01

⚡ Respond within 5 minutes

Studies show leads are 100x more likely to convert when contacted within 5 minutes. Turn on email notifications so you're notified the moment someone submits the form.

Tip 02

📝 Always add notes after a call

Use the Edit modal to add internal notes to every lead after you speak. Future-you will thank you — especially when following up weeks later.

Tip 03

📅 Schedule the next step immediately

At the end of every call, book the next appointment before hanging up. Use the Schedule button in the lead detail panel — it takes 10 seconds.

Tip 04

🎯 Focus on Hot leads first

Start every morning by checking for 🔥 Hot leads. Don't let a freshly submitted, highly detailed inquiry sit until the afternoon.

Tip 05

📁 Export weekly for reporting

Every Friday, export your leads to CSV. Open in Google Sheets to track trends, analyze which services generate the most leads, and plan next week.

Tip 06

🔄 Move statuses religiously

A lead that stays "New" for a week is probably lost. Move statuses as soon as you take action. Your conversion rate stat is only useful if statuses are accurate.

Tip 07

📊 Check the pipeline chart weekly

If too many leads are stuck in "Contacted" with none moving to "Qualified," it's a signal to change your follow-up approach or adjust your offer.

Tip 08

🌡 Nurture Warm leads over time

Not every lead buys immediately. For Warm leads, log a call, add a note, and schedule a follow-up in 2–3 weeks. Consistent touch points build trust.

Configuration

Setup Checklist

Things to configure before going live.

✓

Database is ready

SQLite database (dev.db) is already created and running locally. No setup needed.

1

Change your dashboard password

Open .env and update DASHBOARD_PASSWORD to something strong. Also update DASHBOARD_SECRET to a random string.

2

Set up email notifications

In .env, set EMAIL_USER to your Gmail address, EMAIL_PASS to your 16-character App Password (from myaccount.google.com/apppasswords), and EMAIL_TO to where you want notifications sent.

3

Customize your business name

In .env, update NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_NAME and NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_TAGLINE with your real business details.

4

Deploy to Vercel (optional)

Push to GitHub → connect repo on vercel.com → add all your .env variables in the Vercel dashboard → deploy. Your dashboard will be live at a public URL.
